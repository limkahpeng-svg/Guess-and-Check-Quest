Overview of Concept:  Listing is used when we are given the total value of 2 variables and we are asked to find the individual amount of each variable. Worked Example: Patricia saw 10 ducks and goats in a farm. She counted a total of 26 legs. (a) How many goats were there in the farm? (b) How many ducks were there in the farm? Strategy: Listing Step 1: Make a smart first guess • We need to guess how many ducks and goats there are. Since the total number of animals is 10, we can start by splitting the 10 animals evenly. • 10 ÷ 2 = 5 ( 5 ducks and 5 goats ) No. of ducks No. of legs (ducks) No. of goats No. of legs (goats) Total no. of animals Total no. of legs Check 5 5 × 2 = 10 5 5 × 4 = 20 5 + 5 = 10 10 + 20 = 30 X Step 2 : Adjust our guess to match the number of legs • From Step 1, our first guess gave us 30 legs. However, the actual total number of legs is less than 30, so our guess must be too big. • To reduce the total number of legs, we should adjust the animals that give more legs. In this case, it is the goat. No. of ducks No. of legs (ducks) No. of goats No. of legs (goats) Total no. of animals Total no. of legs Check 5 5 × 2 = 10 5 5 × 4 = 20 5 + 5 = 10 10 + 20 = 30 X 6 6 × 2 = 12 4 4 × 4 = 16 6 + 4 = 10 12 + 16 = 28 X Step 3: Adjust until we get our correct answer • Now the total number of legs is still more than 26 legs, hence our guess is still too big. • Therefore, we still need to reduce the animal with the greatest number of leg. • No. of ducks No. of legs (ducks) No. of goats No. of legs (goats) Total no. of animals Total no. of legs Check 5 5 × 2 = 10 5 5 × 4 = 20 5 + 5 = 10 10 + 20 = 30 X 6 6 × 2 = 12 4 4 × 4 = 16 6 + 4 = 10 12 + 16 = 28 X 7 7 × 2 = 14 3 3 × 4 = 12 7 + 3 = 10 14 + 12 = 26 √ (a) There were 3 goats in the farm. (b) There were 7 ducks in the farm.
